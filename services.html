Our services

Everything you can rent or list, in one place

ReMa covers seven rental categories and the tools to manage them end to end — from the first search to the final payment.

What we cover

Seven categories, each with verified listings and owner-managed availability.

01

Residential properties

Houses, apartments, single rooms, chamber and hall setups.

02

Commercial properties

Real estate, office spaces, co-working spaces.

03

Event venues

Spaces for weddings, funerals, parties and other events.

04

Vehicles

Private cars and buses for hire, self-drive or with driver.

05

Tools and equipment

Working tools and equipment for any job, by the day or week.

06

Furniture

Chair rentals and other event-related furniture.

07

Gadgets

Cameras and other electronics, ready when you need them.

Core features

The platform behind every listing, booking and payment.

Search and filter

Advanced search and filter options to help users find exactly what they need, fast.

User profiles

Profiles for both renters and owners, including ratings and reviews.

Listings

Detailed listings with descriptions, photos, pricing and availability.

Booking system

An integrated booking and calendar system to manage rental periods.

Payment gateway

Secure payment processing for deposits and rental fees.

Customer support

24/7 customer support for any issues or inquiries.

Mobile app

A user-friendly mobile app for both Android and iOS devices, so listings and bookings go wherever you do.
